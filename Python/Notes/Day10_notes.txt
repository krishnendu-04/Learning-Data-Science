BITWISE OPERATORS

    - bitwise and (&)
    - bitwise or (|)
    - "&" does bitwise "and" operation
        - 4 & 5 
        - 100 and 101 ----> 100
    - "|" does bitwise "or" operation
        - 4|5
        - 100 or 101 ----> 101
